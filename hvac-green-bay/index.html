Keeping Your Green Bay HVAC System Running Longer

Many mid-season breakdowns trace back to maintenance that never happened, and the habits that prevent them are simple. Swap your filter every one to three months, keep plants and shrubs at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and hose off the condenser coil every spring. Then book a professional tune-up twice a year, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

How old is your system? That number says a lot about what comes next. Most well-maintained air conditioners make it to 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15. If your system is past that point and also shows rising bills, rooms that never quite reach temperature, or frequent repairs, it's usually due for a planned replacement, which is almost always cheaper and easier on everyone than a rushed emergency install when the first heat wave hits.

If comfort problems do show up, acting quickly keeps them small. A weak capacitor is a quick fix. The same capacitor left alone for a season can take the compressor with it and turn a minor repair into a full system replacement.
