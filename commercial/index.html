Restaurant Services

Restaurant and Food Service HVAC

Restaurants and food-service businesses have climate demands that ordinary equipment can't meet. Kitchens generate enormous heat loads while dining rooms need to stay comfortable, exhaust hoods pull out conditioned air that makeup air units have to replace, and a busy Friday night pushes rooftop units to their limit. Waukesha HVAC specializes in food-service HVAC, including rooftop package units, split systems, correct tonnage and airflow sizing, makeup air balancing against hood exhaust, and scheduled service built around your hours.

Comfort and air balance are critical issues for any food-service operator. A kitchen running negative pressure pulls dining-room air the wrong way, drags outdoor air through every door, and makes staff miserable during peak service. Our work keeps your system delivering the temperatures and airflow your operation depends on, with written service records that show a well-maintained facility to landlords and auditors. You get detailed reports and service logs after each visit, along with the documentation many commercial leases require.

We work directly with owners, managers, and kitchen staff to plan replacements and maintenance that protect your business and fit your operating schedule. Our technicians know how to work in occupied facilities, installing during off-hours when needed, minimizing disruption, and keeping your kitchen running and your dining room comfortable.

Safe Installation

Professional Installation and Documentation

A poorly installed or neglected HVAC system can create carbon monoxide hazards, poor indoor air quality, refrigerant leaks, and expensive water damage from clogged condensate lines. Those are real risks for a commercial property owner with employees, customers, and tenants in the building. Every installation and inspection we perform covers heat exchanger integrity, venting and combustion air, electrical connections and disconnects, refrigerant charge, condensate drainage, and ventilation rates for occupied spaces, and each visit ends with a written report listing risks, priorities, and recommended preventive steps.

Regular professional service protects the people and property you're responsible for, and following through on our recommendations helps keep minor issues from becoming liabilities. It also builds a paper trail. We maintain service logs, temperature and filter records, and a binder of reports for every account, with more frequent visits for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms. When a landlord, insurer, or auditor asks for proof of due diligence, it's ready.
